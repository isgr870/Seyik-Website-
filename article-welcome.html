Welcome to SEYIK – Skilled Youth, Empowered Communities

1 August 2026 · SEYIK

We are Skill & Equip Youth Initiative for Karamoja (SEYIK) — a youth-led organisation working to skill and equip young people for inclusive economic growth while tackling climate change in the Karamoja Sub-Region.

This website is a space where we will share:

  • Updates from our training and community programmes
  • Stories of young people driving change
  • Climate action and advocacy work
  • Opportunities to get involved

Stay connected with us on X (@Skill_Youth_kja) and reach out anytime at s.equipyouth24@gmail.com.
