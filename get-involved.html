Partnerships

SEYIK works through partnership rather than duplication. We are building relationships across four categories:

1. Government

District Local Governments and Community Development Offices in Moroto and Amudat; alignment with the Parish Development Model, Emyooga and the Youth Livelihood Programme, into which our graduates are positioned to enter equipped rather than unprepared.

2. Training providers

Technical institutes, skilling hubs and vocational training centres in Karamoja, into which we place and retain participants.

3. Employers and markets

Contractors, traders, mining operators, livestock market actors and enterprises that can absorb certified young people.

4. Civil society

Karamoja civil society networks and youth coalitions, and national and international organisations seeking credible local implementing partners in the sub-region.
